Tamil Voice-to-Text

Convert voice recordings to Tamil text, right in your browser.

Tap to choose audio files
M4A, MP3, or WAV — drag and drop works too

Short clips (under ~25 seconds) are transcribed instantly. Longer recordings automatically use background processing, which can take anywhere from a few minutes up to an hour or more for very long files. Keep this tab open and your device awake until it finishes. Files must be under 2 hours each — split anything longer before uploading.
